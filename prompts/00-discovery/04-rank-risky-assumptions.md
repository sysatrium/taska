# Prompt — Rank risky assumptions

## Goal

Identify the assumptions whose failure would most damage the product or MVP.

## Rules

- Use the selected segment and jobs as the evaluation context.
- Rank by probability × impact.
- Mark missing data as assumptions instead of blocking progress.
- Propose two to five cheap validation methods per top risk.

## Output

Write the result to `specs/000-project-overview/risks-and-assumptions.md`.
