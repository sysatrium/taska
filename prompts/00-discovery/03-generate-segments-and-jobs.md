# Prompt — Generate segments and jobs

## Goal

Turn discovery context into a shortlist of segment hypotheses and freeze the chosen segment.

## Rules

- Use jobs-to-be-done framing.
- Describe segments by context, role, and success criteria; not by demographics alone.
- Keep one Big Job and one to four Core Jobs per segment hypothesis.
- Output the top three segment options and then the chosen segment.

## Output

Write the result to `specs/000-project-overview/segments-and-jobs.md`.
